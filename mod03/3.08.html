III. Write It

10 min · Right page

  1. Name It: one weakness, one sentence.
  2. Fix It: what you did, step by step. Make this your longest part.
  3. Show It: one real result. “Still working on it” is allowed.
  4. Read it under your breath with the clock. Aim for 45–60 seconds.

IV. Coach It

20 min · Groups of 3

RoleJob
CandidateAnswer the question like you’re in the interview.
Clock + Body CoachPunch in on the second hand. Tally every “um,” “like,” and “ya know.” Watch eye contact.
Structure CoachDid you hear Name It, Fix It, Show It? Pick one fix for Take 2.
  1. Take 1: notebook open is fine.
  2. Coaches give one keep, one fix. “It was good” doesn’t count.
  3. Take 2: notebook closed. No peeking.
  4. Coaches fill in the Coach Box and write an honest score at the top.
  5. Rotate until everyone has played all three roles.
