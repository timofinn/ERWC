Mr. Finn

My greatest strength is a drive to understand systems: how they work, where they break down, and how to fix them when they don’t deliver.

My secret is pretty simple. I treat everything like an experiment. I fiddle with knobs and buttons just to see what they do. That started when I wanted to record my own music at home without paying a sound engineer. Modern recording software is ridiculously deep, so I tinkered until I could run a whole studio by myself.

The cool part is that this mindset travels. It works on almost any problem. As a teacher, I use it every week building curriculum: try something, watch where it breaks, tweak it, try again.

Spot it: where does each move start in my answer? Mark it on your left page.

II. Write It

10 min · Right page

  1. Name It: one strength, one sentence.
  2. Prove It: steal a story from your 3.04 or 3.05 drafts.
  3. Point It: connect it to the job you want.
  4. Read it under your breath with the clock. Aim for 45–60 seconds.

III. Coach It

20 min · Groups of 3

RoleJob
CandidateAnswer the question like you’re in the interview.
Clock + Body CoachPunch in on the second hand. Tally every “um,” “like,” and “ya know.” Watch eye contact.
Structure CoachDid you hear Name It, Prove It, Point It? Pick one fix for Take 2.
  1. Take 1: notebook open is fine.
  2. Coaches give one keep, one fix. “It was good” doesn’t count.
  3. Take 2: notebook closed. No peeking.
  4. Coaches fill in the Coach Box and write an honest score at the top.
  5. Rotate until everyone has played all three roles.
