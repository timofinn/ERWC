3.07 | Make Their Case

❖ ❖ ❖

Purpose

Two trials run in Act Two. One in Salem. One in the kitchen.

Anyone can trash a character. That’s easy.

Today you argue their side so well they’d sign it.

That move is called a steelman. You’ll need it for the mock trial.

Left pageThe Move + Mr. Finn’s model
Right pageSteelman Table + one honest line

I. The Move

7 min · Left page, top

Copy these two lines:

Strawman: twisting or shrinking the other side’s argument so it’s easier to attack. You beat a scarecrow, not the person.
Steelman: building the strongest version of the other side’s argument before you take it apart. You beat them at their best.

Here’s the difference, using Reverend Hale:

Strawman

Hale cares more about being right than being good. He treats Salem as a stage for his expertise, then abandons it the second the performance goes badly.

Steelman

If I were Hale, I’d say: I came to heal a sick child, and I told everyone to trust evidence, not rumors. When I saw the girls were lying, I risked my career to say so. Danforth never admitted he was wrong. I did.

Copy the steelman frame under it. You’ll use it all period:

If I were , I’d say , because .

II. Watch

20 min · Just watch

Watch the film from Hale’s visit to Elizabeth’s arrest. Follow Scenes 3 and 4 below.

While you watch: pick the one person you’d least want to defend. That’s who you’ll steelman first.

Scene 3 · Hale makes a house call. Reverend Hale stops by to warn them that Elizabeth was named in court, and to test them. Why does John skip church? Can he list the Ten Commandments? John gets nine. The one he forgets is adultery. Elizabeth has to fill it in. (Oof.) John tells Hale that Abigail admitted none of it was witchcraft. Hale asks him to say that in court. Then Giles Corey and Francis Nurse burst in: Martha Corey and Rebecca Nurse have been arrested.

Scene 4 · The needle. Court officers Cheever and Herrick show up with a warrant for Elizabeth. Cheever finds the poppet with a needle inside it. Here’s why that matters: Abigail was stabbed with a needle at dinner at Parris’s house, and she says Elizabeth’s spirit did it. Mary admits she made the doll in court and put the needle in herself, with Abigail sitting right next to her. Doesn’t matter. They take Elizabeth anyway. John tells Mary she has to testify against Abigail. Mary is terrified the girls will turn on her. And she knows about the affair.

III. Steelman Swap

15 min · Right page · Partners

  1. Draw the table below. Three rows.
  2. Partner A steelmans Elizabeth out loud. Partner B steelmans John. One minute each.
  3. Listener’s job: would that person sign it? If yes, initial the row. If no, say what’s missing and let them retry.
  4. Write your row in your own words. Then swap characters and do Mary Warren together.
WhoTheir case at full strengthSigned?
ElizabethWhy she still can’t fully trust John
JohnWhy he won’t just go tell the court the truth
Mary WarrenWhy testifying against Abigail is a terrible idea
No villains allowed. If your sentence makes them sound dumb or evil, it’s a strawman. Start over.

IV. One Honest Line

5 min · Right page, bottom

Now you get to judge. Finish this:

The hardest one to steelman was , because .
For the brave: Elizabeth says John is judging himself harder than she is. Steelman that. Is she right?
Last 8 minutes: buffer. Finish your table and get your initials.

Success Looks Like

  • Left page: strawman vs. steelman + the frame 4 pts
  • Right page: 3 steelman rows, each initialed by your partner 6 pts
  • Right page: one honest line 2 pts
