Purpose

Yesterday you drafted STAR stories in keywords.
Today you write 4 full answers, then say them from memory.
Writing it out is how it sticks.

The 4 Questions

  1. Tell me about a time you solved a problem.
  2. Tell me about a time you worked on a team.
  3. Tell me about a time you made a mistake.
  4. Tell me about a time you were under pressure.

Label S, T, A, R in the margin. Full sentences this time.

Partner Recall

  1. Hand your notebook to your partner.
  2. Partner asks 2 of your 4 questions.
  3. Answer from memory. No peeking.
  4. Stuck? Partner says the letter you're on: "You're on Action."
  5. Partner gives feedback. Then switch.

Giving Feedback

Glow: "Your ___ was clear because ___."
Grow: "Next time, add ___ to your ___."

  • Name a letter: S, T, A, or R.
  • Be specific: "the ATM detail," not "good job."
  • One glow, one grow. 30 seconds, tops.

Success Looks Like

  • 4 answers on the left page, every letter labeled
  • 4 answers said out loud from memory
  • 2 glows and 2 grows given
